What does the launch fee cover?+

It covers the agreed design and build scope for your package. We confirm the exact deliverables in writing before work begins.

What does EmberCare cover each month?+

Hosting, SSL, backups, maintenance and the agreed level of practical support. Bigger new features are scoped separately so there are no surprises.

How do payments work?+

Launch fees are paid securely on PayFast. Monthly EmberCare starts only after scope, timing and the care arrangement have been confirmed.

Can I start small and grow later?+

Yes. The packages are starting points. A clear first launch is often the best foundation for future pages, booking, ecommerce or automation.
